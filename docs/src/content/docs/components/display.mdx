Components with a `tone` take `neutral`, `accent`, `info`, `success`, `warning`, `danger` or `important`, each from the theme's status roles.

## Card

<Demo id="card" layout="stack">
  <gw-card heading="Desktop">
    <gw-button slot="actions" size="small" variant="plain">Details</gw-button>
    <gw-facts items='[{"label":"Uptime","value":"3 days, 4 hours"},{"label":"Version","value":"5.2.0"}]'></gw-facts>
  </gw-card>
  <gw-card heading="Open as a link" href="#card">The whole card is the link.</gw-card>
</Demo>

`heading` adds a header, with an `actions` slot beside it. `href` makes the whole card a link, and `flush` drops the padding for a table or image that fills it.

## Chip and dot

<Demo id="chip">
  <gw-chip>Idle</gw-chip>
  <gw-chip tone="success"><gw-dot tone="success" slot="icon"></gw-dot>Online</gw-chip>
  <gw-chip tone="warning">Updating</gw-chip>
  <gw-chip tone="danger">Failed</gw-chip>
  <gw-chip tone="info">Planned</gw-chip>
  <gw-chip tone="important">Breaking</gw-chip>
  <gw-dot tone="success" label="Online" pulse></gw-dot>
</Demo>

A dot on its own needs a `label`, such as "Online"; beside text that says the same, leave it out. `pulse` makes it breathe while something is live.

## Alert

<Demo id="alert" layout="stack">
  <gw-alert heading="Update available">System Bridge 5.3.0 is ready to install.</gw-alert>
  <gw-alert tone="success">Backup finished in 4 minutes.</gw-alert>
  <gw-alert tone="warning" heading="Disk nearly full">Only 8 GB left on the system disk.</gw-alert>
  <gw-alert tone="danger" heading="Connection lost" urgent>
    Can't reach the server.
    <gw-button slot="actions" size="small">Retry</gw-button>
  </gw-alert>
</Demo>

It's announced politely as a status, or at once with `urgent` for an error that needs attention. Slot an icon in `icon` and buttons in `actions`.

## Facts

<Demo id="facts" layout="stack">
  <gw-facts items='[{"label":"Hostname","value":"desktop"},{"label":"Kernel","value":"7.2.8"},{"label":"Memory","value":"64 GB"},{"label":"Uptime","value":"3 days, 4 hours"}]'></gw-facts>
</Demo>

Label and value pairs as a description list. Set `items` to `[{ "label", "value" }]`.

## Meter

<Demo id="meter" layout="stack">
  <gw-meter label="Memory" value="42"></gw-meter>
  <gw-meter label="System disk" value="88" high="80" critical="95" display="880 of 1,000 GB"></gw-meter>
  <gw-meter label="Battery" value="12" tone="danger"></gw-meter>
</Demo>

A bar for a value in a range, with `min` (0) and `max` (100). Past `high` it turns warning and past `critical` danger, unless a `tone` is set. `display` replaces the percentage shown beside the label.
