`heading` adds a header, with an `actions` slot beside it. `href` makes the whole card a link, and `flush` drops the padding for a table or image that fills it.

### Linking from the heading

An `href` link lives in the card's shadow root, so it isn't in the page's HTML and can't take a `target` or `rel`. When it needs to be there, such as a project card opening its repository in a new tab, put an `<a>` in the `heading` slot instead. A click anywhere on the card follows it, except on another control inside, such as a button, and the card shows the link's hover and focus:

  <gw-card>
    <a slot="heading" href="https://github.com/timmo001/groundwork" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Groundwork</a>
    One design and architecture system for every app, CLI, panel, terminal UI and agent.
    <gw-row><gw-chip>TypeScript</gw-chip><gw-chip>Web Components</gw-chip></gw-row>

A chip's `icon` takes SVG path data for an icon before its text, and `swatch` takes a CSS color for a small round dot in its place, such as a tag's own color; for anything else, use the `icon` slot.

<Demo id="chip-anatomy" layout="stack">
  <gw-anatomy>
    <gw-chip tone="success"><gw-dot tone="success" slot="icon"></gw-dot>Online</gw-chip>

A dot on its own needs a `label`, such as "Online"; beside text that says the same, leave it out. `pulse` makes it breathe while something is live.

## Meter

<Demo id="meter" layout="stack">
  <gw-meter label="Memory" value="42"></gw-meter>
  <gw-meter label="System disk" value="88" high="80" critical="95" display="880 of 1,000 GB"></gw-meter>
  <gw-meter label="Battery" value="12" tone="danger"></gw-meter>
</Demo>

A bar for a value in a range, with `min` (0) and `max` (100). Past `high` it turns warning and past `critical` danger, unless a `tone` is set. `display` replaces the percentage shown beside the label.

<Demo id="meter-caption" layout="stack">
  <gw-meter label="/" value="64" high="80" critical="95" display="410 of 640 GB" caption="/dev/nvme0n1p2 · ext4"></gw-meter>
  <gw-meter label="Battery" value="18" inverted high="20" critical="10" display="18%" caption="Discharging"></gw-meter>
  <gw-meter label="Swap" display="-" caption="Not reported"></gw-meter>
</Demo>

`caption` adds a quiet line under the bar. With `inverted`, lower is worse: at or below `high` it turns warning, and at or below `critical` danger, for a battery. Leave `value` unset (or `NaN` from a property) for an unknown reading, shown as `-` with an empty bar. Set the bar's height with `--gw-meter-height`.

## Gauge

<Demo id="gauge">
  <gw-gauge label="CPU" value="23"></gw-gauge>
  <gw-gauge label="Memory" value="81" caption="52 of 64 GB"></gw-gauge>
  <gw-gauge label="Temperature" value="94" unit="°C" warning="80" danger="90"></gw-gauge>
  <gw-gauge label="Battery" value="12" inverted warning="25" danger="15"></gw-gauge>
</Demo>

A reading on a dial. The arc fills to `value` between `min` and `max` and glides when it changes. It turns warning at `warning` (75) and danger at `danger` (90); `inverted` makes lower readings worse, for a battery. `display` replaces the number, `unit` (`%`) sits under it, and `caption` goes under the label. Set its size with `--gw-gauge-size`.
