## App bar

<Demo id="app-bar" layout="stack">

    <gw-button variant="neutral" appearance="plain" href="#app-bar">Components</gw-button>
    <gw-button variant="neutral" appearance="plain" href="#app-bar">Themes</gw-button>
    <gw-theme-picker slot="actions"></gw-theme-picker>
  </gw-app-bar>
</Demo>

A site or app header pinned to the top of the page: a brand in the `brand` slot, nav links in the default slot and icon buttons in `actions`. It runs the full width of the page with its content kept to `--gw-content-width`, and stays transparent until the page scrolls; then it gets a blurred surface and a bottom border, and sets `floating`.

For a page that opens with a hero, set `reveal-after` to a selector for it, such as `[data-hero]`. The bar then stays transparent and hides its brand until the hero scrolls out from under it, so the hero's large logo hands over to the bar's small one. `overlay` lays the bar over the top of the page instead of taking room, so the hero starts at the very top.

Below 600px wide the nav moves into a menu opened from a button, which closes on Esc, a click outside or picking a link. `menu-label` names that button.

```html
<gw-app-bar reveal-after="[data-hero]" overlay>
  <a slot="brand" href="/"><img src="/logo.svg" alt="Home" /></a>
  <a href="/projects">Projects</a>
  <a href="/stats">Stats</a>
  <gw-theme-picker slot="actions"></gw-theme-picker>
</gw-app-bar>
<section data-hero>…</section>
```

</Demo>

| Element | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `gw-page` | Centers a page's content at `--gw-content-width`, or the narrow width with `narrow` |
| `gw-stack` | Stacks its children with the theme's gap |
| `gw-row` | Lines its children up and wraps them; `nowrap` stops that |
| `gw-grid` | Fills columns at least `--gw-grid-min` (18rem) wide |

`gap="s"` or `gap="l"` changes the gap of a stack, row or grid.
