## Spinner and skeleton

<Demo id="loading" layout="stack">
  <gw-row><gw-spinner label="Loading sensors"></gw-spinner> Loading sensors</gw-row>
  <div aria-busy="true">
    <gw-stack gap="s">
      <gw-skeleton style="width: 40%; height: 1.5rem"></gw-skeleton>
      <gw-skeleton style="width: 100%"></gw-skeleton>
      <gw-skeleton style="width: 80%"></gw-skeleton>
    </gw-stack>
  </div>
</Demo>

The spinner is announced by its `label`. A skeleton stands in for what's loading at its size, set in CSS; it's hidden from assistive technology, so mark the region it's in `aria-busy`.

## Toast

<Demo id="toast">
  <gw-button data-toast="Backup finished" data-tone="success">Finish a backup</gw-button>
  <gw-button data-toast="Disk nearly full" data-tone="warning">Fill a disk</gw-button>
  <gw-toaster duration="4000"></gw-toaster>
</Demo>

Put one `gw-toaster` on the page and call `show(message, tone)`. Each toast is announced politely and leaves after `duration` milliseconds. Toasts are for something new that needs the person, not for routine success.
