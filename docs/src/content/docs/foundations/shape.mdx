## Radius

A fixed scale, `--gw-radius-xs` (2px) to `--gw-radius-xl` (16px) and `--gw-radius-pill`, plus three the theme sets: `--gw-radius-control`, `--gw-radius-chip` and `--gw-radius-panel`.

<TokenScale kind="radius" />

## Elevation

`--gw-shadow-s`, `-m` and `-l`, from black at low opacity. Dark modes use stronger shadows, since a shadow reads less on a dark surface. A theme can set their ink and strength with `style.shadow`, as [Writing a theme](/themes/writing-a-theme) shows.
